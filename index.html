01 · Made with

Every model here is built procedurally in the browser with three.js. There are no downloaded meshes. Parts, triangle counts and materials are measured live.

02 · Your privacy

No trackers, cookies, analytics or third-party requests. Fonts and libraries are self-hosted. The only thing stored is your motion preference, and only on your device.

03 · Accessibility

The site respects reduced-motion settings, has a Motion toggle, works fully with the keyboard, and keeps all chapter text readable even without WebGL.
